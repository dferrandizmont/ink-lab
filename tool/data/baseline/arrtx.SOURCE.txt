arrtx_codes.csv
  Hand transcription (code, official name, line, chip position). Sources of
  codes and names, all from the official charts page:
  - OROS 160 codes: the blank OROS 160 card (codes only, no names) and the
    chip image above agree on all 160 codes (sha256 c66aba1e...e871 for
    oros160.jpg):
    https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0742/1755/4203/files/OROS160_1.jpg?v=1764753451
  - names and codes, OROS 90B card (sha256 d551f72b...9ce8):
    https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0742/1755/4203/files/220907_oros_90B_colorsheet_ol.pdf?v=1705379329
  - names and codes, OROS 80A card (sha256 6d77a227...92c0):
    https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0742/1755/4203/files/220906_oros_80_colorsheet_ol.pdf?v=1705379308
  - names and codes, OROS 66 card (sha256 b0ccca65...188e):
    https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0742/1755/4203/files/OROS66.pdf?v=1705379328
  - names and codes, OROS 48 skin tone card: arrtx_oros48_card.jpg above.
  The PDFs draw their text as vector outlines (no text layer): names were
  read from renders at 150 dpi. They are not bundled (about 1 MB each).
  ALP membership (the "ALP" line): ALP 90B, 80A, 40 and 36 cards
    https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0742/1755/4203/files/ALP_90b.pdf
    https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0742/1755/4203/files/ALP_80a.pdf?v=1705116880
    https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0742/1755/4203/files/ALP_40Pastel_Color.pdf?v=1705116865
    https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0742/1755/4203/files/ALP_36skin.pdf?v=1705116872
  and the ALP 66 product page
    https://arrtx.com/products/arrtx-alp-66-alcohol-markers-pastel-color-paint-markers-dual-tip
  (ALP 90B and 80A carry exactly the OROS 90B and 80A codes and names; the
  ALP 66 / 40 codes are the OROS 66 codes; ALP 36 skin tone is 301-334 plus
  the blender.) Arrtx states on the product pages: "ALP 80, ALP 90 and
  OROS 80, 90 colors are the same".
